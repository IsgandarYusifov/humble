What belongs on Humble?

Culture, high-effort meme lore, art, internet IP, AI agents, technical products, and ideas that cross categories. An identifiable creator or team should stand behind the concept.

Is Humble live?

Humble is pre-launch. The first cohort is being prepared; no live launches are listed. You can introduce your idea through Apply.

What does origin mean here?

The purpose is to connect an idea to its creator, its public history, and its canonical address when one exists. Read the approach and its limits.

Can I apply before I have a mint?

Yes. The address field is optional. Start with your idea, the people behind it, and any origin links you can share.
